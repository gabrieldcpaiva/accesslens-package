## Slide 2: The Problem

**Headline:**  
Scanning is not remediation. Remediation is not proof.

**Key points:**
- Automated scanners catch ~30–40% of meaningful barriers
- Most “fixes” are cosmetic and fail on real journeys
- Lawsuits target sites with shallow or unverified remediation
- Teams lack evidence that a repair actually worked

**Stat to lead with:**  
“76% of accessibility lawsuits target sites with incomplete or shallow remediation.”

**Visual:**  
Before/after split — keyboard journey failure vs. pass

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

**Headline:**  
A closed-loop repair system with human authority at every gate

**Flow:**
1. Scoped audit with reproducible evidence
2. Human triage and prioritization
3. Approved-only repair in sandbox/branch
4. Independent verification
5. Before/after evidence package

**Differentiator:**  
Independent verifier cannot see repair rationale. No self-grading.

---

## Slide 4: How It Works

**Headline:**  
Five stages, four checkpoints

**Checkpoints:**
1. **Scope** — domain, journeys, authorization
2. **Triage** — severity, confidence, impact
3. **Repair approval** — exact patch before any change
4. **Release decision** — evidence review before publication

**Demo moment:**  
Live keyboard journey: fail → human approves repair → independent rerun → pass

---

## Slide 5: Proof

**Headline:**  
Trust signals built into the workflow

**Proof elements:**
- Human Authority Ledger
- Confidence labels: Confirmed / Likely / Potential / Blocked
- Before/after journey replay
- Offline deterministic fallback evidence set
- No claims of complete certification or legal compliance

**One-liner:**  
“We don’t ask you to trust the AI. We let you control the decisions—and prove what changed.”
