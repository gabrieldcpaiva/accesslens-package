# AccessLens 48-Hour Launch Sequence
**Objective:** Book 3 scoping calls within 48 hours  
**Assumption:** AccessLens landing page + Calendly/calendar link are live  
**Primary channels:** LinkedIn outbound, Email outreach, Communities

---

## Hour 0 — Cleanup

- [ ] Publish landing page
- [ ] Verify Calendly/scheduling link
- [ ] Create “Audit Blueprint” lead magnet PDF (1-page)
- [ ] Draft 3 outreach templates (LinkedIn, email, community post)

---

## Hours 1–6 — Seed social proof

- [ ] Post AccessLens demo reel/screenshots to LinkedIn/Twitter
- [ ] Post to 2–3 relevant indie-dev/SMB/nonprofit communities
- [ ] Tag 3 peers who might refer pilot clients

**Template:**  
“Built a tool that doesn’t just audit accessibility—it repairs and verifies with human approval at every step. Looking for 3 pilot sites for a fixed-scope engagement. DM if you want the before/after evidence package.”

---

## Hours 7–14 — LinkedIn outbound batch

**Audience criteria:**
- SMB founder with public-facing checkout/booking
- Nonprofit director responsible for public website
- Indie dev shipping a product

**Volume target:** 30 personalized messages

**Message structure:**
1. Specific observation about their site or context
2. One sentence: AccessLens audits, repairs, verifies with human approval
3. One sentence: independent verifier, no self-grading
4. CTA: “Want a free 20-minute scoping call? I’m taking 3 pilots this week.”

---

## Hours 15–24 — Email outreach batch

**Audience:** 50 SMB/nonprofit contacts from public directories or warm leads

**Subject line:**  
“Your accessibility audit probably missed this”

**Body structure:**
1. Hook: most audits don’t catch real journey failures
2. Proof point: 76% of lawsuits target shallow remediation
3. Offer: 20-minute scoping call, fixed-scope pilot
4. CTA: reply or book directly

---

## Hours 25–36 — Follow-up + community content

- [ ] Reply to every inbound within 1 hour
- [ ] Post one case-study-style update: “Here’s what the first pilot showed us”
- [ ] Share before/after screenshot pair with one sentence

---

## Hours 37–48 — Conversion + retarget

- [ ] Follow up with anyone who engaged but didn’t book
- [ ] Offer a narrow time window if calendar is empty: “2 slots left this week”
- [ ] Update messaging based on objections seen so far
- [ ] If no bookings: adjust subject lines / LinkedIn hook / landing page CTA

---

## KPIs to track

- Landing page visits
- CTA clicks
- Scheduled calls
- Show rate
- Pilot close rate

**Success threshold:** 3 scheduled calls within 48 hours  
**Fallback if missed:** sharpen lead magnet or switch outreach list source
