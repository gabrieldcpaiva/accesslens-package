## How It Works

### A closed loop, not a spreadsheet

1. **Scoped audit**  
   You authorize a domain and journeys. We propose boundaries. You approve.

2. **Barrier discovery with evidence**  
   We reproduce real failures—keyboard, form, semantic, visual—with steps and captures, not vibes.

3. **Human triage**  
   You choose what to fix, in what order. We explain impact in plain language.

4. **Approved-only repair**  
   We generate patches. Nothing is changed until you say yes.

5. **Independent verification**  
   A separate verifier reruns the same journey without seeing the repair rationale. Pass or fail—no self-grading.

6. **Evidence package**  
   You get before/after proof, a human decision ledger, regression tests, and a publish-ready summary.

## FAQs

**Does AccessLens modify my live site?**  
No. Default is read-only. Repairs are applied in a sandbox, branch, or patch file. You approve every change before publish.

**What frameworks do you support?**  
Static HTML, React, Vue, and server-rendered sites. Source access improves repair quality, but we also work with URL-only authorized scans and framework-neutral guidance.

**Is this legal compliance?**  
We provide evidence of remediation, not legal certification. No automated system can certify complete accessibility. We are explicit about tested scope and remaining uncertainty.

**What happens after repair?**  
We export tests and monitoring configuration. You can schedule deterministic checks to catch regressions before they become customer issues.
